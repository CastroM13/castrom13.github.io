How it works

Triangle-plane intersections form layer segments; a deterministic planner emits bounded perimeters, infill, travel, and extrusion commands.

Generated G-code is experimental, profile-generic, and must be simulated and reviewed for your exact printer, firmware, material, and safety limits.

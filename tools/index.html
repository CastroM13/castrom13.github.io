The rules of the lab.

  1. Local by default.Your content stays on the device. Required external downloads are disclosed before work begins.
  2. Accessible from the start.Keyboard support, visible focus, reduced motion, clear states, and resilient layouts are requirements.
  3. Explicit limits.Every tool explains what it can verify, what it cannot, and what you should review.

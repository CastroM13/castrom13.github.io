How it works

A disposable LibRaw worker demosaics sensor data and returns fresh 8-bit sRGB JPEG or baseline TIFF output.

Input is capped at 128 MiB and developed pixels at 40 MP; lens profiles, nondestructive RAW sidecars, and original-file mutation are outside this developer.
